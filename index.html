Foodlore

Your household's recipes, the plan of what to cook, and the grocery list that falls out of the plan.

Recipes, from anywhere

Paste a link, paste text, or write a recipe yourself. Every ingredient line is kept exactly as it was written.

A plan and its grocery list

Put meals on a meal plan and the grocery list writes itself: scaled to the servings you are cooking, merged, and sorted by store and aisle.

One household, every phone

For iPhone. There is no account. Your household shares one cookbook, plan and list through your own iCloud, and the developer never sees any of it. Read the privacy policy.
