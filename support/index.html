Foodlore Support

Foodlore keeps a household's recipes, the plan of what to cook, and the grocery list that comes from the plan.

Get help

Write to support@josephgoodrick.com. Say what you were doing and what happened, and include the version shown in Settings under About.

Adding a recipe

On the Recipes screen tap the plus button. A recipe can come from a link to a web page, from text you paste, or you can write it yourself. Every way ends in the same editor, so you can check the recipe before it is saved.

Planning and shopping

Add meals to a meal plan from the Plan screen. Each plan builds its own grocery list from the dishes on it, scaled to the servings you set and sorted by store and aisle. Next shopping trip on the home screen opens the list for the plan that starts next.

Sharing with your household

Open Settings, then Household, then Invite to household, and send the invitation. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone. Once someone joins, you share one cookbook, one plan and one set of grocery lists.

If changes are not showing on another phone, check that both phones are signed in to iCloud and online, then open Foodlore on each.

Using Foodlore without iCloud

Foodlore works in full on one iPhone with no iCloud account and no network. Sync and household sharing need iCloud.

Taking your recipes with you

Open Settings, then Import and export, then Export everything. You get one file with every recipe in the open schema.org and Cooklang formats, along with your photos. The same screen imports a Foodlore export.
