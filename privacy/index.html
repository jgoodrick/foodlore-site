Foodlore Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

The short version

Foodlore collects nothing. The developer has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and cannot see your recipes, plans or lists.

Where your data lives

Your recipes, meals, meal plans, grocery lists and photos are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, Foodlore also keeps them in your own private iCloud storage so they reach your other devices. Apple runs iCloud under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you invite someone. When you invite people to your household or to a meal plan, they can see and change what is in it, including the name you chose in Settings, your recipes, plans, grocery lists and notes. Sharing runs through iCloud. You can stop sharing in the app at any time.

When Foodlore uses the network

  • iCloud sync and sharing, described above.
  • Adding a recipe from a link: your iPhone fetches that web page directly, as a browser would, so that website sees the request.
  • Adding the starter recipes: their photos are downloaded once from Unsplash, which sees the request.

None of these requests go to the developer.

Camera, photos and alarms

If you switch on scanning, the camera is used to photograph a recipe page, and the text is read on your iPhone. Photos you add to a recipe are stored with your recipes. If you switch on timer alarms, Foodlore asks permission to sound an alarm when a cooking timer ends. All of this happens on your device.

Tracking

Foodlore does not track you and contains no analytics or advertising software.

Your choices

You can export everything from Settings at any time, and deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone. Data kept in your iCloud can be managed in the Settings app under your Apple Account.

Children

Foodlore collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions go to support@josephgoodrick.com.
